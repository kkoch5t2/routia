# Routia development

- Keep the app offline and local unless the user explicitly requests a change.
- Habit capacity is fixed at 10 from the first launch, with no capacity setting or editor. Enforce Rules.MAX_HABITS in the UI and the database trigger (habits DB v7). Upgrades from fixed-three or selectable 3–10 versions must preserve every existing goal, record, widget and paid-review choice, remove the obsolete capacity setting, and never implicitly enable paid reviews.
- Minimum achievement counts as success. Preserve accumulated progress. User-authorized optional pledge reminders must never charge money without a separately configured, approved payment integration.
- Never silently downgrade a full achievement through a repeated background action.
- Use immutable, distinct PendingIntents by default. The recording collection template is the sole mutable exception required by Android fill-in click handling: use an explicit non-exported entry Activity (and validate legacy Receiver actions), keep widget/date in the prefilled URI, validate widget ownership and the habit/level payload, and reject stale dates. Notification actions and read-only collection templates stay immutable.
- Check-only quick actions must not open the main app. Numeric recording requires a focused input Activity; one numeric value is sufficient. Widget collection clicks use an explicit validated Activity template, with mini check routing finishing without UI and a newly completed goal allowed to show its dedicated celebration. Do not launch Activity from a notification BroadcastReceiver.
- Do not publish personal data, signing keys, passwords, SDK paths, or device IDs.
- Keep release signing material outside the repository.
- Run domain tests and Android lint after relevant changes. Use the separate integration database for storage tests.
- UI smoke tests are emulator-only and require an empty test app. Never clear a real user's app data.
- Report build verification, emulator verification, and installation on physical devices separately.
- Do not make unsupported claims about dopamine, exact habit-formation periods, or guaranteed success rates.

- When changing widget sizing, inspect version-qualified XML overrides and verify the installed provider dimensions on a modern Android device.

- Progress widget summaries must contain only current streak and cumulative actual achieved days on one line. Do not label historical or monthly success as today. Date refresh must not write achievement records.
- Keep Cream visibly warmer than White, and keep the added Black palette neutral gray/black in widget and app surfaces.

- Time/count habits derive achievement from numeric input. Do not ask users to select an achievement grade in numeric forms or widgets. Preserve legacy history; old numeric grade-only quick actions must not create new records.

- Check criteria use ミニ達成 → 目標達成 → チャレンジ達成, in ascending order, and all three are always available. All setup fields are optional: use the visible generic HabitDefaults on blank input. Never invent domain-specific criteria or numeric targets. Preserve explicit existing criteria and historical records.
- Celebration requires check level >= 2, or a first numeric crossing of an explicitly configured positive target. Mini, below-target, targetless, repeated totals, and correction-only saves must not automatically animate. Read-only replay must also meet current eligibility.
- Keep supporting amounts smaller than habit titles in the app, entry sheet, achievement screen and widgets. Numeric correction must expose a confirmed removal action. Use the shared emoji/slash streak summary.

- Ruti is the recurring squirrel companion: daily small actions accumulate like an acorn reserve. Use the shared Mascot assets across icon, headers, explanations, empty states, entry/timer, widget previews and celebrations. Keep destructive confirmations focused.
- Celebration is an articulated performance: independent head, torso, arms, feet and tail, two claps, raised arms, a small vertical hop, grounded landing and alternating steps. Preserve the fixed root/foot anchor and continuous transforms without lateral drift. Keep source atlas neighbors clipped out. Test contacts/continuity and review native rendered frames.

- Keep shoulder pivots inside the opaque torso, upper arms behind the torso and distal forearms/paws in front. Verify native alpha-pixel connectivity across the full motion, including the final still, and inspect black and cream native frames; coordinate-only tests cannot detect detached artwork.
- All three achievement backgrounds are solid and borderless in recording and correction; retain the grade palette, checked radio indicator and touch feedback.

- Ruti has compact two-bone arms and small separate paws. Keep physical upper-arm/forearm lengths fixed, use upright forearms for the raised segment, and keep claps at chest height. Never rotate a single long arm cutout as the whole limb. Inspect resting/open-clap/contact/raising/raised poses.
- On the celebration screen, emphasize the current streak in large bold text and place a small lifetime total under the weekday ribbon. Keep the emoji/slash single-line summary in other screens and widgets.

- Raised-arm reference: elbows outside the torso with upper arms near horizontal; wrists above elbows, forearms nearly vertical, paws upright. Turn the forearms up before lifting the upper arms. Do not validate only unsigned elbow angles or enforce one 2D bend sign across a 3D outward turn. Keep physical bone lengths fixed while allowing projected foreshortening. Test the hands-above-elbows relation through the entire raised segment and final still.

- Cover the entire jaw/scarf seam with an opaque fur underlap behind both layers. Whole-body connectivity alone cannot detect holes at the neck. Verify neck opacity throughout the animation, including head tilts and expression changes, and review black/cream renders.


- AI report requirements: four concise tabs (要点 / 傾向 / 改善策 / 根拠), local factual charts, and native flow diagrams. Offer shared レポートの詳しさ (簡潔 / 標準 / 詳細), default 標準. Match generation instructions, per-field schema and total validation to the selected depth; ranges guide useful detail, never force padding. Preserve saved report depth and use the legacy standard contract for reports without depth metadata. Avoid generated HTML.
- The user explicitly authorized optional external AI report integration. Keep normal recording offline; AI requests require separately configured personal credentials. Never embed a key, publish a credential, send raw notes/device identifiers, or silently enable paid generation.
- Report budgets are user-selected ceilings of 100–1000 JPY equivalent per report in 50 JPY steps, default 300, not fixed charges or targets to exhaust. Account for input and reasoning/output before sending. Keep currency/fee assumptions explicit. Stop on unknown charges and never automatically resend an uncertain paid request.
- Use a separate reports database and preserve the existing habit records. Distinguish recorded facts, personal hypotheses, and cited research. Do not invent session times, interruption reasons, scientific sources, or causal findings.
- Report QA uses application ID suffix .reportqa on an emulator. Never clear or seed the production app for report testing.

- Shared report settings: API key/model/budget have exactly one settings form. Goals opt in independently and default off; a disabled goal must not start paid generation. Preserve per-goal activation dates, cancel unsent work on disable, and recheck options before sending. Store the actual model and budget on each report; do not invent missing metadata for old reports.
- Present report expense as the user-selected commitment consequence. Padded price examples are scenarios, not measured charges. Never imply a higher ceiling guarantees a larger bill or spend tokens just to exhaust it.

- User-facing report name is 継続レビュー. Use 金額の目安 for the pricing label. Shared settings buttons have no emoji. Put plain full-width 戻る beneath Save, matching other settings forms; the financial commitment purpose belongs in explanatory copy.

- Keep the weekly review-budget scenario prominent above the selectors and beside Save, with its formula and the distinction between target week and billing week. Show a manual-review control and unmet prerequisites at the top of Overview; never silently enable a goal or start a manual paid request merely from saving/opening settings.

- Review settings QA must cover a fake encrypted key and an enabled goal in the same UI save, plus persisted network-job registration and cancellation. Network-constrained JobScheduler work requires ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE in the manifest. Keep system/storage errors distinct from invalid input; never log credential values or raw exception messages.

- Label AI-generated advice explicitly and keep pre-generation copy and locally computed facts distinct. New advice must explain why it fits the person; configured cues are plans, not evidence of successful execution. Context/cue is optional and must not force every intervention into an if-then plan. Preserve old report JSON.
- Manual paid generation must show fee/model/cap confirmation before enqueue or retry. The Settings tab owns a persistent global review switch. Turning it on requires explicit cost/target/model/cap/weekly-budget confirmation; cancel must leave it off. Only when globally on, every settings save with enabled targets must confirm automatic fees and weekly budget, even when unchanged or using save-and-open; per-goal enabling then also requires consent. While globally off, allow preparatory settings/target saves without fee consent and never turn the master on implicitly. Gate automatic/manual enqueue, scheduling and final submission on the master; cancel all unpaid queued/counting work on global off, preserve submitted/unknown requests and saved reviews, and retain individual choices/key/options. Reenable automatic judgment from tomorrow without replaying the off period. New installations default off; preserve existing approved active targets on upgrade. Serialize preference changes with enqueue and final paid-submission transitions to prevent off/on races. Cancellation must not persist drafts, keys or manual requests. Preserve approved manual options and stop before paid submission if those settings change.

- Keep review section titles larger than AI prose. Use 18sp bold section titles, 16sp regular advice, and matching 13sp bold AI/context labels across all four tabs. Do not inflate prose above its section title.

- Review charts must include a completed as-of day and exclude an unfinished as-of day from failure denominators. Recalculate display weekdays from daily snapshot entries so legacy closed-day arrays cannot hide achievements. Replace the contribution grid with numeric daily-amount lines or check-habit cumulative achieved-day lines; preserve unknown amounts and unit mismatches as gaps. Keep saved-report snapshot timing explicit.

- State the 28-day display window explicitly on review curves. Show the main header date with year and the full Japanese weekday. Achievement period/granularity/navigation must be independent per habit and persist without resetting another habit or forcing scroll to top.
- Open both review and shared configuration as full-screen Activities with shared header, margins, card styles and bottom full-width 戻る. Protect the configuration Activity with FLAG_SECURE, preserve encryption and paid consent, and clear unsaved key text when leaving. Keep previous periods, tabs and scroll positions when returning. Correction-grade indicators need a real drawable inset, not just text padding.

- Calendar empty/future cells must blend into the active background in every app palette. No grid lines, cell strokes or striped rows. Achievement and ticket colors are identical across all backgrounds and match the shared fixed palette. Only empty/future cells adapt to the background. Use light green for mini, medium green for goal, dark green for challenge; numeric quantity also increases from light to dark. Legends must match the displayed calendar. Ticket-use cells are red without a ticket emoji; compact calendars keep normal centered date labels, and calendar legends/help/accessibility describe the red cells.

- Cost guide ranges are hypothetical planning scenarios, not measured charge distributions or guaranteed bounds. Keep model/depth selectors, manual controls and paid confirmations synchronized via ReportBudget. Distinguish scenario estimates, selected spending ceilings and maximum processing reservations; disclose normal-input/cache and FX assumptions.

- In the model selector show the aggregate cost-guide span across all report depths from the start. Keep depth choices, selected previews and paid confirmation specific to the selected model/depth.

- Keep paid continuing reviews on their own main navigation tab between Achievements and Settings, selectable by task and actual completion year/month, newest first. Use database pages of ten saved metadata rows, with a fixed previous/jump/next control set and a separate wrapping status line; never enumerate every page number. Scope month options to only months with completed reports in the selected task and local year, plus All (never list empty months); keep the month menu disabled with only its placeholder for All/unknown year. Map month values independently of spinner positions and reset unavailable month/page state when task/data changes. Scope year options to saved timestamps, handle old unknown timestamps explicitly, and preserve/clamp page/filter state across returns and recreation. Reject out-of-range or overflowing jump input without dismissing the dialog. Put a visible upper-right diagonal 有料 ribbon on that tab in selected and unselected states; its slope runs from upper left to lower right, and accessibility announces the paid label. Keep Achievements focused on its summaries and free chart entries. Preserve unknown completion dates explicitly for legacy reports. Keep task summaries visible with a fixed current-month rollup and explicit month labels. Show week/month/year selectors and previous/next period controls only on the free chart screen; chart choices must not change the summary period; open free local graphs on their own non-exported screen rather than below paid reviews. Preserve filters, scroll and independent periods when returning or recreating. Reading saved reviews must never generate or charge. The AI review itself opens immediately readable; keep its shared configuration and Back accessible. Notify only after saved automatic completion and route to that exact report with immutable intents. Use 💰️ for existing continuing-review emoji. Place the Today saved-review button in each habit action area immediately above 今日の達成を見る when present (above entry/timer controls for numeric habits). Today links use actual completion timestamps in the local day; never infer dates for legacy reports.
- Step-up suggestions require the local progression gate and are optional. Prefer continuity/recovery, retain minimum success, respect a maximum 5% candidate and cooldown/milestone rules, never change goals automatically, and never add paid milestone-triggered generation. Product thresholds are not scientific/medical claims.

- Daily reminders allow 0–10 distinct local times (0 disables daily reminders), default five at 12:00/15:00/18:00/20:00/22:00. Preserve customized legacy times; migrate untouched three-slot defaults, invalidate/cancel old alarm revisions and all removed slots, and retain same-day per-slot deduplication. No fixed three-slot indexing in delivery or Today. Use dynamic chronological scheduling, immutable intents, explicit duplicate/bounds validation and current date/revision checks. Keep the count/time editor scrollable with fixed Save/Back and preserve draft/picker state across recreation.
